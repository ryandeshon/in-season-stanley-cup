# Expressive portrait assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using each corresponding `src/assets/arcade/*-approved.png` as an identity-preserving edit reference. Existing approved images remain the loading/error fallback. The four selected outputs are saved as `src/assets/arcade/{ryan,cooper,boz,terry}-expressions-v1.png`.

Each file is one square atlas. The app displays one equal quadrant at a time using CSS background positioning: top-left Happy, top-right Angry, bottom-left Anguish, bottom-right Sad. This is a state atlas, not a full-body animation sequence. Elemental impacts are separate PixiJS and CSS layers. The generated sheets were visually reviewed, including face, costume, framing, grid placement and defeated-state damage.

## Generation prompt set

Common instructions: identity-preserve; create a production expression atlas of the exact original custom character in the reference. Single square image subdivided into exactly 2 by 2 equal square tiles, no gaps, no borders, no labels or text. Each tile is an independently usable square chest-up portrait with matching camera, scale, shoulder line, head location, background, palette and lighting. Match the chunky detailed 1990s pixel art. Preserve the facing direction across all tiles. Top-left: composed confident ready expression. Top-right: angry, furrowed eyebrows, tense or clenched jaw and tightened guard. Bottom-left: anguish at impact, eyes squeezed shut and pained grimace, slight recoil without leaving the tile. Bottom-right: sad/defeated, lowered gaze, disappointed brows and slack shoulders. Make expressions clearly distinct at small UI sizes. No elemental effects baked in, no extra characters, no gore or logos. Quadrants meet exactly at x=50% and y=50%. Request 2048×2048 if possible.

Character-specific constraints:
- Ryan: black and gold armor, tight shaved sides, swept brown hair, short beard, red throne room; all tiles face left.
- Cooper: brown hair and beard, bright blue eyes and intact black rectangular glasses, broad silver/blue conical hat, blue and white armor and gloved fist, icy blue arena. Happy has a closed mouth and slight smile; Angry shows frustration; Anguish closes eyes behind intact glasses; Sad keeps his face visible. Preserve safe hat margins.
- Boz: swept dark hairstyle and shaved sides, beard, black sunglasses, black/dark green outfit with white piping, gold spotlights. Happy has a cocky grin and hand at sunglasses; Angry raises a defensive fist; Anguish has a pained open mouth with sunglasses still on; Sad loses his swagger and drops his shoulders. Express emotion through mouth and brows above glasses.
- Terry: spiky brown hair, stubble rather than a full beard, green eyes, green scaled shoulder armor, black glove and dark red trim, green corridor; all tiles face left. Happy has a cunning smirk; Angry tightens his guard; Anguish flinches; Sad lowers his guard.
